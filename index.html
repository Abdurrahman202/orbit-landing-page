Find your rhythm.
Keep it simple.

You do not need a perfect routine. Just a small place to begin.

    Choose a task you can give your attention to. Everything else can wait.

  2. 02

    Give it a little time.

    Start a focused session and settle into your work, one small step at a time.

  3. 03

    Pause. Then begin again.
